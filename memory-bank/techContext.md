# Tech Context

This document details the technologies used, development setup, technical constraints, and dependencies.

## Technologies Used

- **Primary Tool:** `AIDevX`, an internal, web-based wrapper for a Large Language Model (likely ChatGPT).
- **Core Skill:** The training focuses on AI Prompt Engineering, a methodology for interacting with LLMs.
- **Deliverable Format:** The training content will be created in Markdown (`.md`) format for easy conversion into slides or teaching notes.

## Development Setup

- No specific development environment is needed. The work involves research, content creation, and fact-checking. A web browser for research and a text editor for writing the content are sufficient.

## Technical Constraints

- The prompting techniques must be compatible with the assistants available in `AIDevX`, such as "Generate Requirements," "Create User Manual," etc.
- The content must be generated without access to the live `AIDevX` tool; therefore, the examples will be based on the provided descriptions of its capabilities.
- The final content must be comprehensive and self-contained ("don't limit your token").

## Dependencies

- The accuracy of the training material depends on the list of `AIDevX` assistants and their functions as provided in the initial brief.
- The effectiveness of the training relies on up-to-date, fact-checked information about best practices in prompt engineering.
