# AIDevX Workshop: Trainer's Notes & Facilitator's Guide

## A. Workshop Overview

- **Total Time:** 90 Minutes
- **Objective:** To equip BAs and SAs with advanced, practical prompting techniques to generate high-quality, structured documents using AIDevX, aligned with Malaysian public sector standards (**KRISA**).
- **Materials:** Projector, Slides (`training_slides.md`), Whiteboard/Flip-chart (optional for activities).

### **Target Deliverables for this Training**

This workshop is specifically designed to help Business Analysts and System Analysts use AIDevX to accelerate the creation of the core documents required by the KRISA methodology. Throughout the session, all techniques and activities will be framed around generating content for these four key deliverables:

1. **Spesifikasi Keperluan Bisnes (SKB) / Business Requirement Specification (BRS)**
2. **Spesifikasi Keperluan Sistem (SKS) / System Requirement Specification (SRS)**
3. **Spesifikasi Rekabentuk Sistem (SRB) / System Design Specification (SDS)**
4. **Supporting Artifacts**: Including Business Process Models (e.g., BPMN diagrams), Use Case Models, and Data Models.

## C. Detailed Section Guides

### **Section 1: Professional Transformation Journey**

- **Slides:** 1-2
- **Time:** 10 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Welcome participants and position this as professional skills development, not just technology training.
  - **Frame the Challenge:** Acknowledge the reality they face - weeks to create comprehensive KRISA documents, multiple review cycles, formatting challenges.
  - **Professional vs Amateur:** Walk through the comparison table showing how professional methodology transforms both process and outcomes.
  - **AIDevX Platform Overview:** Explain specialized assistants and demonstrate the workflow transformation from 2-3 weeks to 2-3 hours.
  - **Key Message:** "You're not just learning AI tools - you're developing professional methodologies that will accelerate your career and project success."

### **Section 2: Expert Persona Strategy**

- **Slide:** 3
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Introduce the "Professional Consultant" analogy. Emphasize that you hire consultants based on specific expertise and experience.
  - **Impact Demonstration:** Walk through the before/after comparison showing generic vs. government-specific output.
  - **Strategic Selection:** Explain how different KRISA documents require different expert personas with relevant domain knowledge.
  - **Government Context:** Show how persona specificity ("15 years Malaysian government digital services, KRISA-compliant documentation, PDPA compliance") transforms output quality.
  - **Key Insight:** "The more specific and relevant your expert persona, the more professional and applicable your document output."

### **Section 3: Expert Selection Workshop**

- **Slide:** 4
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Activity Instructions:**
  1. Present the Ministry of Health E-Prescription System scenario - emphasize the complexity and real-world stakes.
  2. Walk through each document type (SKB, SKS, SRB) and the three expert options provided.
  3. Have participants call out their choices and justify their reasoning.
  4. **Discussion Focus:** Connect expert selection to document quality and compliance readiness.
  5. **Key Outcome:** Participants understand that expert persona selection directly impacts project success.
- **Trainer's Tip:** Use this as an opportunity to reinforce that government projects require government-experienced experts.

### **Section 4: Project Intelligence Framework**

- **Slide:** 5
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Introduce the "Executive Briefing" analogy. Emphasize complete information transfer for professional results.
  - **The Information Gap:** Explain that AIDevX has extensive general knowledge but zero project-specific context.
  - **Context Transformation:** Walk through the DBKL digital permit system example showing generic vs. contextualized requests.
  - **Context Architecture:** Review the three essential categories: Project Foundation, Stakeholder Ecosystem, and Operational Constraints.
  - **Professional Insight:** "Context quality directly determines output quality. Comprehensive context enables government-ready documentation."

### **Section 5: Context Intelligence Workshop**

- **Slide:** 6
- **Time:** 10 mins
- **Activity Instructions:**
  1. Present the government project context analysis challenge with the inadequate brief.
  2. **Step 1:** Individual analysis (3 mins) - have them identify specific context gaps for government SKS.
  3. **Step 2:** Professional collaboration (5 mins) - pairs develop comprehensive context framework with four categories.
  4. **Step 3:** Expert validation (2 mins) - one pair presents how complete context transforms AIDevX output.
  5. **Professional Outcome:** Demonstrate understanding that context completeness correlates with document quality.
- **Trainer's Tip:** Emphasize how context gaps in government projects lead to unusable deliverables and project delays.

### **Section 6: Professional Template Methodology**

- **Slide:** 7
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Introduce the "Master Craftsman" analogy. Emphasize learning through perfect examples rather than descriptions.
  - **Quality Transformation:** Explain how government documents require precise formatting and how template examples eliminate interpretation errors.
  - **Template Methodology in Action:** Walk through the Jabatan Imigresen visa system example showing format description vs. template example approaches.
  - **KRISA Requirements:** Show the complete template with compliance notes and proper government formatting.
  - **Professional Principle:** "One perfect example is worth a thousand words of description."

### **Section 7: Template Creation Workshop**

- **Slide:** 8
- **Time:** 8 mins
- **Activity Instructions:**
  1. Present the template transformation challenge using raw notes about system admin user role management.
  2. Show the target KRISA format that participants need to achieve.
  3. Have pairs create a complete prompt incorporating expert persona, context, and template examples.
  4. After 7 minutes, ask one pair to present their solution, highlighting the professional approach to template-driven formatting.
- **Trainer's Tip:** Use this to reinforce how template methodology ensures consistent, government-standard formatting across all requirements.

### **Section 8: Blueprint Planning Method**

- **Slide:** 9
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Introduce the "Architect's Approach" analogy. Emphasize that complex documents require planning before construction, just like buildings need blueprints.
  - Rationale: For large KRISA documents, direct generation often misses critical elements. Blueprint planning ensures comprehensive coverage and allows validation before detailed writing.
  - **Real Example:** Walk through the FPX payment integration scenario. Show how blueprint planning for Bank Rakyat identifies all components, integrations, and security considerations before writing the SRB.
  - **Key Message:** "Professional documentation requires professional planning. We're not just generating content - we're architecting solutions."

### **Section 9: Architecture Planning Workshop**

- **Slide:** 10
- **Time:** 10 mins
- **Activity Instructions:**
  1. Present the real government scenario: Jabatan Akauntan Negara's E-Claim system for 50,000+ civil servants.
  2. Emphasize the complexity: "This isn't just a simple expense system. It's a government-wide platform with integration requirements, compliance needs, and audit trails."
  3. Have pairs work through the planning framework, focusing on system ecosystem mapping and integration architecture.
  4. Ask one pair to present their blueprint planning approach. Highlight the professional thinking required for government-scale projects.

### **Section 10: Professional Document Refinement**

- **Slide:** 11
- **Time:** 5 mins
- **Talking Points:**
  - Introduce the "Master Chef's Method" analogy. Emphasize that professional quality requires iterative refinement, just like a master chef perfects a dish.
  - Walk through real Malaysian government examples showing vague vs. professional guidance.
  - **Government Standards Focus:** Explain that government documents require multiple review cycles. Show how specific feedback like "add PDPA compliance details" or "include integration with HRMIS" transforms generic content into government-ready documentation.
  - **Key Message:** "Government work demands government standards. We guide AIDevX like we would guide a junior analyst - with specific, actionable feedback."

### **Section 11: Document Quality Workshop**

- **Slide:** 12
- **Time:** 10 mins
- **Activity Instructions:**
  1. Present the Universiti Malaya student portal scenario with the vague requirement.
  2. Run through the structured 3-step exercise: individual brainstorming, pair collaboration, and class sharing.
  3. Guide the group through professional requirement standards: specific actors, precise actions, business constraints, and compliance notes.
  4. Have one pair present their refinement approach and discuss why it meets government standards.
- **Trainer's Tip:** Emphasize the real-world impact - vague requirements lead to project delays and budget overruns in government projects.
