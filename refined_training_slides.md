### 📍 **Slide 11: Technique 4 - Chain of Thought Prompting (Blueprint Planning)**

**🎨 Design:** Architect with blueprints planning
**📷 Image Keywords:** "architect blueprints," "planning before building"

#### **Like an Architect Planning a Building**

For complex documents (SRB with multiple sections), make AI plan first before writing.

*🔍 **Research Deeper:** Search online for "chain of thought prompting" or "step-by-step reasoning" for advanced techniques*

#### **❌ What Most People Do**
```
"Generate complete SRB for mobile payment system"
```
**Result:** Generic document missing critical components

#### **✅ What Professionals Do**
```
You are a Lead Solutions Architect at Bank Rakyat Malaysia. 

Before creating the technical SRB document, please blueprint the FPX 
integration by analyzing:

STEP 1: Component Architecture
- Which existing bank systems will be involved?
- What new components need development?

STEP 2: Integration Touch Points  
- How will mobile app connect to core banking?
- What APIs are required for FPX gateway?

STEP 3: Data Security Framework
- What customer data flows through the system?
- How do we ensure PDPA compliance?

After you blueprint this, I'll review before you write the full SRB.
```

#### **🛡️ AI Safety Tips:**
- ⚠️ **Limit to 3-5 planning steps maximum** to avoid overwhelming AI
- 🔍 **Verify:** Review the blueprint before requesting full document
- 🚨 **Red Flag:** If AI skips steps or combines multiple phases
- 📋 **Best Practice:** Save the approved blueprint to re-reference later
