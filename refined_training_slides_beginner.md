**🎨 Design:** Chef tasting and improving dish
**📷 Image Keywords:** "chef improving recipe," "step by step refinement"

### **Like a Chef Perfecting a Recipe**

Don't expect perfect output on first try. Improve it step by step with specific feedback.

### **❌ What Most People Do**
```
"This BRS needs work"
```
**Result:** AI doesn't know what to improve

### **✅ What Professionals Do**
```
REFINEMENT 1:
"Thank you for the initial draft. Let's improve the Business Objectives section 
to be more specific and SMART-compliant. Please rewrite to include:
- Measurable targets: Increase student achievement by 15% in 2 years
- Specific numbers: Serve 2.5 million students in 10,000+ government schools  
- Cost savings: Reduce physical textbook costs by 30%
- Policy alignment: Comply with Malaysia Education Development Plan 2013-2025"

REFINEMENT 2:
"Good improvement. Now enhance the Stakeholder Analysis by adding:
- Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia (KPM) - Policy maker and regulator
- Jabatan Pendidikan Negeri - State-level policy implementer  
- Teachers - Primary users for content delivery
- Students - End recipients of digital learning"
```
